# Plan Completeness Policy v2

Plan requirements are proportional to the request. The Engine derives booleans for timeline, stages, tasks, priority, and feedback loop.

A weekly plan requires at least two time periods or stages, concrete tasks attached to them, and a review/adjustment loop. A question about what to do today requires concrete tasks and priority but not an artificial weekly timetable.

Deterministic checks recognize natural time and sequence language, not only weekday labels. Retry diagnostics name exact missing parts: `missing_timeline`, `missing_stage_structure`, `missing_task_assignment`, `missing_priority`, and `missing_feedback_loop`. The retry Directive places these requirements before optional Phrase instructions. noFit never removes plan structure.

Only plan, comparison, image, and Assistance are eligible for controlled Soft Task Completeness review when deterministic evidence is genuinely ambiguous.
