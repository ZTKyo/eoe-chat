# Live-Safe Phrase Policy v2

Live-Safe is a quality designation, not a quantity target. A Phrase must be frequent, simple, reusable, context-comprehensible, safe in bilingual syntax, resistant to labels and duplicate translation, and unable to replace the user's task.

Activation requires:

- two natural examples from different topics;
- at least one successful real-Provider example before human activation approval;
- explicit grammatical role and allowed positions;
- forbidden patterns and noFit conditions;
- at least two general bilingual frames;
- no dependence on a Corpus ID or canned answer.

`p-maybe` must not be inserted as “这里 maybe …” or used instead of a real stance. `p-step-by-step` must not start a sentence, act as a heading, or appear as “按 step by step 的节奏”. `p-take-a-closer-look` requires substantial follow-up analysis. Sentence-initial `p-in-the-long-run` must be capitalized and recorded as `sentence_start`.

The v2 review prioritizes `I think`, `it depends`, `that makes sense`, `for example`, `for now`, `a little`, and `kind of`. Individual phrases may remain in the registry while being excluded from Live-Safe.
