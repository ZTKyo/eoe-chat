# Natural Overlay Policy v2

Priority order:

1. Complete and accurately answer the user's task.
2. Fulfil Engine-owned Response Obligations.
3. Preserve an ordinary, natural conversational voice.
4. Optionally realize the selected Phrase without changing items 1–3.
5. Return a precise `noFit` result when natural realization is unavailable.

The Provider must not shorten or restructure an answer for a Phrase, use a Phrase as a title or label, replace analysis with a Phrase, repeat-translate it, teach it automatically, or expose control state. A user-authored English phrase may be naturally reused without increasing overall difficulty or adding a second expression.

Naturalness is a hard eligibility gate. Coverage is a ceiling, not a quota. Independent human review is the authority for product naturalness.
